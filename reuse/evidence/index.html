Reuse evidence request

This is the MRT-3 request point for works published under the 0PN Machine Reuse Terms 1.0 (version reference 0pn-mrt-1.0-2026-08-31). The machine-readable descriptor for this point is at /reuse/evidence/index.json.

The automated request endpoint is not yet live. Until it is, send your request by email to info@tcieg.org. You are not required to identify yourself to receive the provenance record. No account, email verification, or identification is a condition of a response.

What you can ask for

Under MRT-3, the party that ingested a work must be able to produce, on request, the provenance record MRT-2 requires: where the work was obtained, when it was obtained, and the licence and terms in force at the time of collection, referenced by version.

Request format

Include these fields:

work (required)
The URL of the work, its terms_version_reference, or a content hash.
dataset_id (optional)
Where you already know which corpus is in question.
reply_token (required for the automated endpoint)
An opaque token you generate, at least 128 bits of entropy. The response is retrieved by presenting this token, so no return address is collected. For the interim email route, a reply address is only what you choose to share.

Response

The response states whether the work was ingested, the source URL, the date obtained, the licence and terms version at collection, the corpus identifier, whether attribution metadata was retained, the accountable responder, and the response date. A refusal, with a reason, is a valid and attributable response. The target response time is 30 days.
