== English ==

ITCoP Badge notice
Before you sign up, here is what this collection involves.
Controller: TCIEG, the Transparency and Consent Interoperability Expert Group, operated by TCIEG US (Transparency and Consent Interoperability Expert Group, LLC, a Florida limited liability company, document number L26000502069). Its Controller Identification Record is at tcieg.org/.well-known/transparency/cir.json.
Authority: TCIEG US is established in the United States. If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK, the EU GDPR or the UK GDPR applies (Article 3(2)) and you can complain to your data protection authority. An Article 27 representative is not yet appointed.
What is collected: only the email address you enter.
Purpose: badge signup and support for the ITCoP 2028 campaign for a Digital Transparency Charter.
If you turn on campaign updates: your email is also used to send you TCIEG campaign updates, until you unsubscribe.
Lawful basis: your consent, given by submitting this form.
Where it is stored: a server in the European Union (Helsinki, Finland).
Your rights: you can withdraw consent or ask to remove your entry at any time, as easily as you gave it, with your receipt ID or by writing to info@tcieg.org.
Notice receipt: a receipt is issued to you on signup.

0PN Notice, Level 1: 0PN Public Registered
Level 1, 0PN Public Registered, is the next rung above Level 0, 0PN Public. It records a full inspectable controller entry for an organisation.
Controller: TCIEG, operated by TCIEG US (Transparency and Consent Interoperability Expert Group, LLC, Florida). When listings open, your organisation record is passed to the 0PN Registry, operated by Open Consent Group Ltd, which is then the controller of your listing.
What is collected: organisation name, country, organisation website, privacy contact, and an online access point URL if you have one.
Purpose: to activate the transparency signal and add it to your badge.
Validation: the organisation's valid legal status is checked against a public registry when the backend is live.
Where it is stored: a server in the European Union (Helsinki, Finland).
Your rights: you can withdraw or ask to remove this organisation record at any time, as easily as you gave it, with your receipt ID or by writing to info@tcieg.org.

Avis 0PN, niveau 1 : 0PN Public Registered
Le niveau 1, 0PN Public Registered, est l'échelon suivant après le niveau 0, 0PN Public. Il enregistre une fiche de responsable complète et vérifiable pour une organisation.
Responsable du traitement : TCIEG, exploité par TCIEG US (Transparency and Consent Interoperability Expert Group, LLC, Floride). À l'ouverture des inscriptions, la fiche de votre organisation est transmise au registre 0PN, exploité par Open Consent Group Ltd, qui devient alors responsable de votre inscription.
Ce qui est recueilli : nom de l'organisation, pays, site Web de l'organisation, contact vie privée et une URL de point d'accès en ligne le cas échéant.
Finalité : activer le signal de transparence et l'ajouter à votre badge.
Validation : le statut juridique valide de l'organisation est vérifié auprès d'un registre public lorsque le service est en ligne.
Où c'est stocké : un serveur dans l'Union européenne (Helsinki, Finlande).
Vos droits : vous pouvez retirer cette fiche d'organisation ou en demander le retrait à tout moment, aussi facilement que vous l'avez donnée, avec l'identifiant de votre reçu ou en écrivant à info@tcieg.org.
